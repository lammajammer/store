# Business/legal items to resolve before taking live payments

This is a build checklist, not a substitute for professional legal/tax advice.

1. Seller identity: decide whether the seller is Dad individually, a DBA, or an entity. Stripe and policies must match reality.
2. Business registration/DBA: check state/local requirements for the seller's actual location.
3. Sales tax: configure Stripe Tax or a deliberate manual approach after confirming nexus/registration obligations. Do not assume Stripe registering/filing is automatic.
4. Product IP: sports team/league logos are protected marks. A disclaimer does not grant permission. Do not sell counterfeit goods or represent replicas as licensed/authentic without substantiation.
5. Product descriptions: document condition, size and inclusions accurately.
6. Return/refund policy: publish before checkout and apply consistently.
7. Shipping: handling time, carrier responsibility, tracking, lost/damaged packages.
8. Privacy: disclose Stripe, Google, Vercel, Make, OpenAI and any marketing platforms that receive customer or uploaded data.
9. Email marketing: transactional order emails are different from marketing. Obtain proper consent before promotional email.
10. Security: 2FA on Google, Stripe, GitHub, Vercel and Make. Never put API keys in browser JavaScript or GitHub.
11. Chargebacks: retain listing snapshot, order record, shipping receipt and tracking.
12. Accessibility: keyboard navigation, contrast, form labels, descriptive alt text.
13. Product photography: use Dad's own photos or images he has rights to use.
